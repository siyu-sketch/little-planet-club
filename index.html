OUR STORY

A world worth
caring for.

Little Planet Club is a student-led environmental club concept for young people who want to reconnect with nature and make sustainable living feel simple, social and meaningful.

Stay curiousNotice the world around you.
Live mindfullyMake room for better habits.
Grow togetherCare for the places we share.

THREE WAYS TO CARE / FIELD GUIDE

Find your place

Find your own way to care for Earth. Our proposed activities are designed for students, whether you are new to sustainability or already exploring it.
